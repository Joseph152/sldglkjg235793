Read before you speak

Overview

The core things to actually remember, above every individual drill.

1. Slow down and take pauses
This fixes people not understanding you, gives you more time to think, and makes you more confident.
2. Relax your jaw, shoulders, and neck
A tense body is not a strong voice.
3. Use hand and face gestures
This is what gets you out of a monotone voice.
4. A simple answer beats a novel
Sometimes a simple answer is better than a novel. And if you can't reason with someone, remember — Jesus tried convincing people, and no words or arguments got through. That's not necessarily a failure on your part.
5. Let statements end low, not high
Ending a sentence with a rising pitch (uptalk) makes even a confident point sound like a question — like you're asking permission instead of stating a fact.
